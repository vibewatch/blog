# Cloud Migration Checklist

Use this checklist to prepare a migration and confirm that the workload is ready for cutover:

*   **Inventory:** Identify applications, owners, dependencies, data volumes, and current performance baselines.
*   **Target environment:** Confirm identity, networking, access controls, monitoring, and regional capacity.
*   **Data:** Choose a transfer method, verify backups, and test restoration and data integrity.
*   **Validation:** Test functionality, performance, security, and estimated operating costs in the target environment.
*   **Cutover:** Agree on downtime, success criteria, responsible owners, and a tested rollback plan.
*   **Operations:** Verify alerts and support procedures after migration; retire the old environment only after acceptance.
